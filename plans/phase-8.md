# Phase 8: Accept the interleaved community, not a feature checklist

## Objective
Verify that the integrated population produces the specified connected lives without human direction and remains a community when a visitor joins and leaves.

## Implementation
- Depends on phases 1–7. Fix cross-activity conflicts/repetition in existing components, not another planner/framework.
- Evaluate `plans/bot-community.md`'s unchanged common contract with the distinct authority routes below and scratch evidence. Separate setup, behavior, durable outcomes and presentation; no new endpoint/controller or general QA infrastructure.
- Six accounts prove capability, not crowded-server density or authorization to expand.

## Verification
- Run nonempty `community_` regressions/project gates; retain phase-local controller/native negatives. API responses/elapsed `run` time cannot prove culture.
- Execute the ordinary zero-Human campaign through the focused in-process route below, not prepared actor `run`. Keep protected API capability checks and the registered-native visitor journey separate. Follow the common seeds/budgets, not invented market/Cellar goals.
- Review linked outing/resource, social/economic, downtime and earned-event sequences against declared contexts/rejections. Include failures, quiet spans, counterfactuals and all attempts. Missing natural evidence or repetitive combat/template invitations fails the relevant gate.
- Normal-client visitor interacts/refuses/leaves; independent life continues. Present ordered native frames/receipts or live review against owner windows, then user authenticity review. Neither counts/logs nor highlights substitute; video is optional.

### Ordinary campaign: account lifetime, not selected actors
All execution here is future acceptance, not authorized during planning.

- Reuse phase 1's focused file-backed App/World/Store integration fixture beside `src/server/account_actions/tests.rs`. In each fresh isolated database call existing `bots::start(&app)` once, the same six-profile entry used by `server::serve` with `shore_bots`; this reaches `runtime::launch(selected=None)`. Do not create prepared characters, register/spawn API actors, assign activities/assets/acquaintances, or manually respawn. No listener/native connection is opened in this focused case; fixture reads are evaluator-only, never bot knowledge or commands.
- For the campaign initialize its Directory with existing production `Directory::with_seed(data, content, max_players, seed)` before `Handle::spawn`, using seeds 7/42/101 in separate fresh runs. Reuse fixture plumbing, not its fixed `test_with_seed(12351)` generated Realm: ordinary Realm sources/progression remain authoritative. Record seed, source/layout identities and initial account/character bindings. These seeds control Realm selection/name through `realm_rng`, not every World/dungeon RNG, account-seeded Brain or asynchronous ordering; report that limitation rather than promise deterministic replay. Do not enable `prepared_seed` or reseed live worlds/controllers to manufacture outcomes.
- Let existing `Handle::spawn` advance ordinary 50-ms Directory ticks while the actual controller loops run. In the focused case service `World::pending_progress` with Store batch/World completion acknowledgements and `World::pending_deaths` through existing `server::persist_death`, matching `server::serve`'s persistence coordinator; do not just mark Store characters dead. No action-driving fixture damage or forced event progression belongs in this ordinary campaign; phase 1's forced-death case stays contextual.
- Use six consecutive bounded time windows per run, each at most 600000 ms of unpaused `app.world.clock.now()` time, for the common sixty-minute game-clock budget. Record Directory tick deltas as advancement evidence, not elapsed-time success. Set a declared wall deadline per window (game budget plus 30000 ms); poll health/lifetime/receipts at intervals no greater than 1000 ms throughout. Timeout, stalled clock/ticks, lost controller/output, quarantine or persistence failure ends and preserves the failed attempt, never silently restarts it. Window boundaries only checkpoint evidence; do not reconstruct minds, respawn or reset the world. Pause for evaluator inspection only with the shared Clock, exclude paused time, and retain pause/resume boundaries. Shut down and await departures/commits/ownership release at the end, as in phase 1.
- Track by the six account identities, not an initial actor/player ID: retain every account→character→world/player/session binding, transfer, pending/committed death, departure and replacement entry, including the ordinary three-second replacement gap. After committed death, `runtime::launch(selected=None)` must obtain its own `shore_character` and enter the replacement. Reconcile grave/dead-character unloadability, starter/legitimate Vault holdings and account activity/history against Store receipts as in phase 1. A missing replacement or stale-session effect fails; fixture-created replacements cannot repair the run. Natural death/reunion absence is unobserved here and remains covered by focused contextual cases, not induced for a campaign count.
- Establish continuous zero-Human provenance structurally: from before `bots::start` until final shutdown this App has no serving listener, native client or other entry producer; only the ordinary six-account entry and its own transfers/replacements can enter. Retain that setup, complete entry/exit/controller-change traces and timestamped all-world controller/health snapshots throughout every window and lifecycle gap, checking no Human/Qa or unrecognized account and one autonomous owner per account (allow the documented death gap, not an unexplained lost loop). Capture evaluator observations from current Directory/World views and committed Store outcomes through existing in-process fixture access, never actor-registration-only `observe`/`inspect`. Missing trace coverage or a Human connection invalidates zero-Human evidence; a connected motionless observer is not an exception.

### Prepared capability and native presentation are separate
- Existing protected `spawn(game_bot)` calls `start_test`→`start_selected`; `observe`/`inspect`/`run` require that spawned registration, and `run` fails when its selected character dies (`src/server/testing.rs`). Use those controls, bounded `time` goals (≤600000 ms), catalog/trace/receipts only for phase-local prepared living-character capability/negative checks. Preserve selected-death termination; neither rebinding an actor nor spawning its successor establishes the ordinary campaign. No new registration endpoint or implicit fallback route.
- Run normal-client visitor/presentation checks separately through the existing registered native bridge, private display and coordinated lease/pause rules. They can show the connected lives required above, including refusal and continued activity after departure, but cannot supply evidence for the zero-Human span. Prepared event finales, successful API calls and selected-character survival never substitute for ordinary earned progression or the common interleaved-community gates.
