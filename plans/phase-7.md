# Phase 7: The population participates in shared events

## Objective
Ordinary world progression creates collective outings and varied, consequential boss aftermaths.

## Implementation
- Depends on phases 1–6. Extend `src/bots.rs`, `src/bots/runtime.rs`, `src/bots/pursuits.rs`/`src/bots/adventures.rs` using closure/Castle/Oryx/Cellar lifecycle and phase-6 Lab. Add missing shared rules only after target-version checks.
- Known cues, readiness and observed peers inform cautious advance, voluntary entry, remedy/help/advice and ordinary combat/travel.
- Actual own loot/no-loot drives inspection, short reports, phase-4 markets/phase-5 redistribution, lingering or exit—not synchronized celebration.

## Verification
- Common contract: red–green `community_events_` cases vary danger, knowledge, readiness, opening, loot and audience. Assert legal travel, real item use/damage-credit/rewards/remedy; no fabricated loot speech.
- Controllers progress through Castle/Chamber/Cellar and Lab from labeled initial contexts. Stopped front player/loot pause cannot synchronize everyone; changed readiness alters participation/exit. Native evidence shows cautious advance, real status/loot and divergent aftermaths.
- Earned closure/event progression must also occur in the unprepared campaign; finale teleports cannot satisfy self-sustaining event culture.
