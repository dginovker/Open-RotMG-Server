# Phase 5: Help, acquaintances and Nexus life persist

## Objective
Specific interactions create selective social continuity, including help/refusal, legitimate rebuilding and meaningful downtime.

## Implementation
- Depends on phases 1–4. Extend `src/bots/social.rs`, `src/bots/pursuits.rs`, `src/bots/runtime.rs`/`src/store.rs` with bounded factual acquaintance episodes, not fictional friends or proximity-based trust.
- Use guarded trade/drop and existing healing: selective gifts/help, refusal and acknowledgment after real effects. Own needs may override generosity.
- Connect rebuilding with Nexus capacity waiting/conversation, private equipment time and ordinary departure/reentry; preserve caps/unlocks and distinguish downtime from failure.
- **Separate approval required before implementation:** the following narrow operation expands shared testing controls; `/create-plan` or approval of bot behavior does not authorize it. If declined, stop and resolve an existing-fixture alternative rather than silently adding a setter.
- Proposed protected starting-context operation in existing `src/server/testing.rs`/`scripts/game.mjs`: lower the active Realm's `max_players` before enrollment. This does not exist at the pin. Require authenticated prepared test state, the existing serialized mutation gate, paused clock, no ownership claims or players, and a one-time integer limit `1 <= limit < App.max_players`; reject invalid, repeated or late requests explicitly. Apply only to the current active Realm through the Directory, synchronize its normal Realm portal publication and return the target/limit in the prepared receipt/capabilities. No arbitrary-world/live capacity setter, server-wide capacity change, closure override, replacement-Realm inheritance or general QA infrastructure; ordinary worlds retain existing defaults.

## Verification
- Common contract: red–green `community_social_` cases cover ignored/refused/helped requests, HP/MP/cooldown/inventory effects, acquaintance versus proximity, unavailable friend, death/restart and stale contact.
- Native end-to-end: help changes a later choice; gifts are accepted/refused; real death leads to legitimate rebuilding/account recognition or independent continuation. Use normal runtime enrollment: selected-character QA bots stop at death and cannot prove replacement.

- Feasible starting context: global capacity remains at least six; use the protected one-time setup above to lower only the active Realm to four. Enroll the existing six-account cohort, four living `game_bot` controllers in that Realm and two in Nexus within ordinary portal-observation range. Resume actual controllers through existing protected bounded time runs. Require a sustained observed `4/4`, unusable entrance while one controller waits/socializes and another reconsiders its own activity. Trace their received public cues, choices and subsequent guarded actions; no privileged capacity receipt or hidden occupancy reaches their minds.
- An occupant must then depart through its own ordinary Nexus/Vault travel decision, not fixture removal, teleport, kick, forced `act` or capacity increase. Require the normal portal publication to change `4/4`→`3/4` and unusable→usable, reach a waiting controller's observations, and cause reconsideration and a legitimate entry attempt. Match observation/decision order to departure/entry receipts, world membership and the return to `4/4`; never exceed four Realm occupants or the unchanged global ownership cap.
