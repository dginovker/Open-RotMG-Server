# Phase 1: People keep their own projects

## Objective
An account pursues a meaningful personal project through interruptions and world changes instead of restarting its personality on entry.

## Implementation
- Add bounded account minds/activities in new `src/bots/pursuits.rs`, integrated with `src/bots.rs`, `src/bots/runtime.rs` and `src/store.rs`. Replace upper flat arbitration with need/opportunity → activity → interruption/resumption/end; keep guarded travel, combat, inventory and World/Store authority.
- Retain assessment/combat machinery, not identical competence: replace the unconditional tier-upgrade shortcut (`src/bots.rs:555–580`) with familiarity/evidence-based assessment; feed bounded attended observations and execution estimates into tracking, aim and movement scoring (`src/bots/combat.rs`). Keep exact physics, legal actions and persistence authoritative; vary neither server truth nor arbitrary action suppression.
- Persist account interests, factual experience and activity identity/progress outside `enter`. Reconcile every resumption against committed holdings and current observations; bind effects to originating account/character/session. Transfers rebind a continuing activity, never old actions. Death invalidates character-bound steps; restart invalidates queues, receivers and deadlines, not durable account purpose.

## Verification
All checks below are future acceptance under `plans/bot-community.md`'s common contract, not work to run during planning. Red–green `community_pursuit_` tests must include actual-controller contextual cases, not only isolated mind decisions; use existing protected controls/native bridge, never Playwright or new general QA infrastructure.

### One supply project, with continuity that banking cannot fake
- Starting context: a living Wizard has no Health Potions (`0xa22`), seven occupied backpack slots including a bankable spare, usable Vault space and a reachable, actually observed bag containing two Health Potions. Its own supply interest derives a two-carried-potion reserve for the next outing; the fixture supplies context, not a scripted activity or hidden bag knowledge. No dungeon clear is required.
- Controller sequence: assess the observed supply opportunity, commit pickup of one potion, then interrupt collection because the backpack is full. Travel normally to Nexus/Vault, commit banking the spare while retaining the potion, and return through a legitimately available Realm entrance. If the original Realm/bag remains reachable, navigate by remembered observed area, reobserve the bag and collect only the missing potion. Never reuse a prior-world object ID as current sight. Declare a feasible bag lifetime/route before the positive case; an expired bag or unavailable original Realm is the negative case, not permission to inject a replacement bag.
- Require one durable activity ID and account throughout: trace observed opportunity/evidence, deficit 2→1, capacity interruption, originating and rebound world/player/character/session, bank commit, resume with deficit 1, then pickup commit and end reason. Match these to ordered World inventory revisions and Store character/Vault holdings. The same activity must survive each `enter` and keep its committed partial progress; a new equivalent activity, reset deficit, fresh pickup credit or merely Realm→Vault→Realm fails.
- Satisfaction means the live character actually carries at least two usable Health Potions after commit; banked/spoken/promised quantities do not count. Already-satisfied starting holdings produce no supply outing. On reobservation, missing/expired loot or inaccessible opportunity ends that step explicitly and leaves the unmet reserve available for legitimate replanning, with the predecessor/reason recorded. Death invalidates the old character's collection, not an assertion that its potions survived. Failed/lost commit remains uncredited until reconciled; duplicate/stale/old-session outputs cannot alter progress or holdings.
- A normal-client sequence should show inspection, a partially completed supply errand, space-making and purposeful return—or an intelligible abandonment when the opportunity is gone. This is the material continuity behind C04/C09's supply/inspection life, not a claim that the owner footage depicts this exact errand.

### Equal-resource paired controller outcomes
Use equal class, level, stats, HP/MP, gear, backpack/Vault means, scene and decision cadence; vary one cognitive factor at a time, with no hidden evaluation facts entering either controller. Assert both the assessment/skill inputs and committed outcomes, then review rendered sequences. These are contextual contrasts, not permanent novice/expert roles.

| Factor | Skill-level difference and paired outcome required |
| --- | --- |
| Knowledge/familiarity | With the same compatible unfamiliar drop, a familiar account can justify a useful equip from learned item facts; an unfamiliar account inspects or tries it safely before deciding. Include a supported reconsidered swap as well as a sensible upgrade. Trace changed evidence/assessment and committed equipped slots, not an identical immediate tier comparison with extra waiting. |
| Attention | During concurrent item inspection and a crossing projectile pattern, broad attention maintains threat tracks while assessing the item and chooses a safe movement; narrow, task-occupied attention omits/stales a particular track and chooses a different path, producing a registered hit or later escape in the declared scene. Assert which public cues were attended and how they changed clearance/movement inputs. Merely postponing the same omniscient dodge fails. |
| Execution | With identical attended cues, knowledge, risk and intended attack/escape, practiced tracking/aim and finer candidate evaluation yield a hit and a safe passage; coarser velocity/aim/path estimates yield a miss and a registered hit in the paired scene. Assert different aim/movement and World damage/HP receipts at equal decision cadence. Shared exact `predicted` physics/guardrails may remain, but identical full-view `clearance`/movement and aim plus different reaction delays cannot pass. Errors must follow bounded estimates, not random disabled shots/dodges. |
| Risk | With the same correct threat assessment and execution, a cautious account abandons the exposed pickup and commits an earlier Nexus escape; a more risk-tolerant account attempts the pickup while exposed and incurs the declared damage/late escape. Trace the common danger estimate and differing acceptance boundary. Do not mislabel risk preference as worse attention or require every risky choice to kill. |

Retest with changed item/location/hazard and without competing load: inspection must end, narrow attention can handle a lone cue, and weaker execution must still complete a simple shot/path. Failure includes unchanged low-level assessment/combat, cosmetic pauses, random incompetence or resource differences explaining the contrast. This grounds C02/C05/C06/C09's uneven competence, sensible upgrades and costly overload without reenacting a clip.

### Real lifetime routes, including death and restart
- Use protected `game_bot` fixtures for living-character transfer/interruption pairs only. They call `start_test`→`runtime::start_selected`; that selected controller exits after committed death and must not be described as automatically controlling a replacement. Preserve the existing `selected_character_death_never_creates_a_replacement` assertion.
- For ordinary replacement, add focused in-process `community_pursuit_` integration cases beside the existing server tests: reuse the file-backed App/World/Store fixture pattern in `src/server/account_actions/tests.rs`, call existing `bots::start(&app)` (the `server::serve`/`shore_bots` entry for the existing six profiles), and drive ordinary world ticks/damage. Process `World::pending_deaths` through existing `server::persist_death`, as production does; do not substitute `Store::death` alone or a fixture-spawned replacement. The real `runtime::launch` loop with `selected=None` must depart and enter its own `shore_character` replacement. No new endpoint, controller type, population or general harness.
- Assert committed grave/dead-character unloadability, same account/new character and session, normal starter holdings plus only legitimate account Vault resources, invalidation of old character steps, retained account interest/history, and no old-session action/duplicate output applied to the replacement. The existing `death_committed_during_departure_rebuilds_in_realm_without_resurrection` test anchors the transfer race; extend with actual-loop coverage, not helper-only proof. Rebuilding can begin a linked new character activity, never inherit the dead activity's collected potions.
- Restart case uses that same ordinary entry: interrupt the partially completed supply activity, signal shutdown, await departures/commits and ownership release, then reconstruct App/World/Store on the same test database with fresh output channels and call `bots::start` again. Assert same account/living character, durable activity ID/partial progress and factual history, fresh session binding and reconciled holdings; no replayed pending action, stale bag handle or volatile timer. If the bag/world no longer exists, require explicit opportunity invalidation/replanning, not invented continuity. Repeat with death already committed before restart: no resurrection or duplicate replacement. Read reopened Store state as well as traces; stopping/restarting only `Brain` does not pass.
- Pair contextual controller evidence with normal-client observation locally. These checks establish continuity and distinguishable cognition only; crowds, fuller resource/storage choices, markets/installments, selective help/acquaintance, Nexus/private life and shared events remain the later phases of the unchanged common vision.
